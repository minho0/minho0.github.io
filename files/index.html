About

I build reliable robot systems for real-world autonomy. My work is on service-robot software stacks — ROS 2 navigation, RGB-D and point-cloud perception, traversability and costmap generation, and behavior-tree-driven task execution with recovery. Individual capabilities are mature in isolation; what still breaks is the integrated system in an open, cluttered home. That gap is what I work on.
