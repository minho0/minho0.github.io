Building robot systems that can understand situations, recover from failures, and finish long-horizon tasks.

I am especially interested in modularizing HRI, perception, navigation, and manipulation capabilities and coordinating them through Behavior Tree-based execution with explicit preconditions, success conditions, failure reasons, and recovery policies.
